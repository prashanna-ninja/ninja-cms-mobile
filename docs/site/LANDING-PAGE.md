## 2. Support page `/support`

A light page with the shared header (wordmark on a short navy band) and footer.

- **h1:** Support
- **Lead:** We're here to help with the Ninja CMS app and adviser portal.

**Contact us**
Email **support@ninjacms.com.au**. Include the email address you sign in with and what you were doing when the
problem happened. We reply on Australian business days.

**Common questions** (an accordion or plain list; `<details>` is fine)

- **How do I get an account?**
  Accounts are created by your organisation. Ask your practice or licensee administrator to invite you. You'll
  receive an email to set your password.
- **I forgot my password.**
  On the app's sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?** and we'll email you a link to set a new one.
- **I belong to more than one organisation.**
  After signing in, choose the organisation you want to work in. You can switch at any time in **Settings**.
- **How do I delete my account?**
  In the app, go to **Settings → Delete account**, or see
  [Deleting your account](/privacy#delete-account) in our privacy policy.
- **How is my information protected?**
  See our [privacy policy](/privacy).

**Meta:** title `Support · Ninja CMS`; description `Get help with the Ninja CMS app and adviser portal.`
